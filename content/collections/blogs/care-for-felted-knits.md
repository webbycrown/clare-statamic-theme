Felted merino does not like a hot wash. Lay the knit flat, reshape the hem, and let it dry away from a radiator.

A light steam brings the surface back if it looks tired. Do not hang a heavy knit on a hanger overnight. The shoulders will stretch.

If a pill appears on the sleeve, use a comb, not scissors. The cloth is meant to last more than one winter.
