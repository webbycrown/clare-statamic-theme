Wool layers well when the pieces get thinner as they move out. A fine merino next to the skin, a felted knit over that, then a coat that does not fight the sleeve.

Avoid two bulky knits at once. That is how shoulders look tight and cuffs ride up. Clare liners and half-zips are cut so they stack without extra bulk.

If a sleeve feels tight, size the coat first. The knit can usually stay as it is.
