Kids grow. Buy the quilt jacket with a little room in the sleeve and a hem that still covers the back when they reach up.

Wash it inside out and skip the tumble dryer if the fill looks tired. A line dry keeps the shape.

The Clare kids jacket is tagged with coats and outerwear so you can find it next to the adult layers.
