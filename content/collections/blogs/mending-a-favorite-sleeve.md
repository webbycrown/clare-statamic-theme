A worn cuff is not the end of a knit. Turn it, stitch the thin spot, and keep wearing it.

We would rather you mend a favourite sleeve than replace a piece that still has years in the body.

If the elbow goes, a small patch in the same wool looks honest. Hide it only if you want to.
