A soft tote packs flatter than a structured bag and still holds a week of errands. We like one that takes a sweater, a book, and a small laptop.

The Clare micro tote is small on purpose. If you need more room, use it inside a larger holdall rather than buying a bag you only fill once a year.

Roll knits. Fold shirts. Keep shoes in a cloth bag so leather does not mark the wool.
