A longer coat changes how you walk through a cold street. The hem blocks wind, the collar sits close, and you stop thinking about the weather.

We cut Clare coats with a clean shoulder and enough cloth to layer a merino knit underneath. The point is not a loud silhouette. It is a coat you forget you are wearing until you step inside and feel warm.

If you are choosing a first city coat, start with navy or stone. Those two colours sit over almost everything in the catalog.
