A quiet shoe does more for an outfit than a loud one. Clare Point 3 shoes are made to sit under a coat and trousers without pulling the eye down.

Pick a leather that can take rain. A pale suede looks good in a photo and poorly on a wet pavement.

If you walk a lot, size for the sock you actually wear in winter, not a thin summer pair.
