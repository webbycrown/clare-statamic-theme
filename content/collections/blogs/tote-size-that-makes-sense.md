Most totes are too wide. They swing, they sag, and a laptop sits crooked. A smaller tote that stands closer to the body is easier on a commute.

Measure the bag against the books and the machine you actually carry. Then stop. Extra pockets rarely get used.

The Clare tote is cut for that daily load, not a weekend away.
