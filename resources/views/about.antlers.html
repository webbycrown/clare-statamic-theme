About

It pains me physically to see woman victimized,rendered pathetic by fashion.

Clare started as a small studio making coats and knits that last more than one season. We cut clean shapes, pick honest fabrics, and keep colour quiet so the clothes sit easily in a real wardrobe.

Every piece in this catalog is chosen for daily wear: city coats, merino layers, soft trousers, and bags that hold a week of errands without looking busy.
