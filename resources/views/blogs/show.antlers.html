A longer coat changes how you walk through a cold street. The hem blocks wind, the collar sits close, and you stop thinking about the weather. That is the Clare idea: clothes that get out of the way.

We pick cloth first, then the shape. Wool that does not itch, cotton that does not cling, and leather that softens instead of cracking. The catalog is small on purpose so each piece earns its place.

How we edit the catalog
  • A clean everyday piece with a quiet colour and a fabric that holds up through the week.
  • Start with a coat, add a knit, then one bag that takes a laptop and a book.
  • Keep colour quiet. Navy, stone, black, and rust last longer in a wardrobe than a print you tire of in a month.

“ A good coat should disappear once it is on. You notice the weather less, and you stop adjusting the collar.”

If you are unsure about size, send an inquiry with your usual fit. We reply with measurements and a close match from the catalog.

When the week is busy, wear the same coat, swap the knit, and keep the bag light. That is how most of our catalog is meant to be used: fewer pieces, worn more often.
