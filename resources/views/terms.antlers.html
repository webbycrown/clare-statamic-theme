Terms & conditions

1. About this catalog

Clare is a clothing catalog for coats, knits, trousers, bags, and everyday accessories. You can browse products, read the journal, and send an inquiry. This demo does not take payments, create orders, or ship goods.

2. How inquiries work

Use the contact or product inquiry form if you want size advice, a similar style, or more detail on cloth. We reply by email. A reply is information only. It is not a sale, a reservation, or a confirmed order.

3. What this site does not do

  • There is no cart, checkout, or payment form.
  • Prices are sample catalog figures for browsing.
  • Stock, colour, and size can change.
  • There is no order tracking or download area.
  • Your message is used only to answer the inquiry.
  • Ask if you need another size, colour, or a close match.

4. Using the content

Product photos, prices, and journal notes are shown so you can explore the Clare look. Please do not treat them as a live offer to sell. If you reuse any text or image, credit Clare and keep the meaning intact.

5. Prices and availability

Every price on Clare is a sample figure so the catalog feels complete. It is not an offer to sell and it is not tied to a live warehouse. If a piece matters to you, send an inquiry and we will tell you what is current.

We may update a title, photo, or price when the catalog is refreshed. Older screenshots can go out of date. The page you see today is the one that applies.
